Official OBubba visual identity

This is OBubba, the mum-built baby tracker and parent support app.

The OBubba visual identity pairs the star baby mascot with a glowing baby sleep timer, moon, sun, coloured time arcs and golden fireflies. In OBubba, fireflies are a symbol of hope: each one represents another parent awake at the same time.
