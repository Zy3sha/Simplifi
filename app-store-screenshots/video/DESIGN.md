## Style Prompt

OBubba app preview video: calm premium baby-care product, readable at phone scale, emotionally warm without feeling clinical. The video should feel like a tired parent immediately understands what the app does: learns their baby, predicts naps, explains sleep, supports feeding/weaning, helps carers and protects parent wellbeing.

## Colors

- Ink: `#2F2734` for day-mode text
- Cream: `#FFF8F1` for night-mode text
- Blush: `#C07088` for care and parent support
- Mint: `#6FA898` for growth and reassurance
- Violet: `#7B68EE` for sleep intelligence
- Gold: `#D4A855` for weaning and night warmth
- Night: `#07101F` for night-mode overlays

## Typography

- Use Avenir Next or the closest available system rounded sans.
- Headlines are direct, large and parent-facing.
- Supporting copy is short enough to read in 2 seconds.

## What NOT To Do

- Do not imply NHS, WHO or AAP endorsement.
- Do not use medical-certification language.
- Do not cover the entire app screen with marketing text.
- Do not use tiny captions or dense paragraphs.
- Do not use loud music/audio; App Store preview should work muted.
