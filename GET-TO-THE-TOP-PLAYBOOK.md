# OBubba: Get to the Top — The Playbook
**29 April 2026**

---

## The Honest Starting Point

OBubba has: zero reviews, zero social following, zero ad spend, zero press coverage.

The #1 app (Huckleberry) has: 5M+ users, $700K/month revenue, $16M funding, 4.9 stars with 57K reviews, NYT/TechCrunch coverage.

**But here's what they DON'T have:** TikTok presence, emotional brand voice, NHS-backed guidance, a working Android app, milestone tracking, or any real advertising. The door is wide open.

## Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-4)
*Cost: £0-500. Goal: Get the basics right before spending a penny on ads.*

### 1.1 Fix the App Store Listing (Day 1)

Your listing is the #1 conversion factor. Get this wrong and everything else is wasted money.

**Title (30 chars max):**
```
OBubba: Smart Baby Tracker
```

**Subtitle (30 chars max):**
```
Sleep, Feed & Nappy Log
```
*(Don't repeat any word from the title. Each word is a keyword.)*

**Keyword field (100 chars, no spaces after commas):**
```
breastfeeding,newborn,nursing,nap,pumping,growth,milestone,routine,schedule,toddler,weaning,bedtime,wake,teething,development,NHS
```

**Screenshots:** Lead with BENEFITS, not features:
- "Know exactly when baby needs to nap" (not "Nap timer")
- "See why baby is crying — ranked by your data" (not "Cry analyser")
- "Track feeds, sleep, nappies — one tap" (not "Logging screen")
- First 3 screenshots are critical — they show in search before anyone taps

**Description first line:** "Built by a mum at 3am. OBubba learns your baby's unique rhythm and tells you what's coming next."

### 1.2 Review Engine (Day 1)

You need 100 reviews at 4.5+ stars ASAP. This is the single biggest ranking factor.

**Strategy: Pre-prompt filter**
1. After 7 days of use, show: "How's OBubba working for you?" with Love it / Needs work
2. If "Love it" → trigger `SKStoreReviewController` (official Apple review prompt)
3. If "Needs work" → open feedback form (catches complaints before they become 1-star reviews)
4. Target: 5-10 reviews per week, steady stream (algorithm prefers consistency over bursts)

### 1.3 Referral Program (Day 1)

Parents tell other parents. Make it effortless.

**Mechanic:** "Share OBubba with a friend → you BOTH get Premium free for a month"
- Trigger after a positive moment: "You've logged 30 days! Share with another parent?"
- Pre-written WhatsApp/iMessage with personalised link
- Double-sided reward (referrer feels like they're giving a gift, not selling)
- Lemon8 paid $0.12 per verified signup and got 10.2M users in one quarter

### 1.4 Submit for Apple Featuring (Day 1)

**How:** App Store Connect → Featuring Nominations. Submit 2+ weeks before target date.

**What Apple looks for:**
- Beautiful UI following Human Interface Guidelines
- Innovation (AI sleep predictions, NHS-backed guidance = unique)
- Accessibility features
- Use of Apple tech (widgets, Siri, Dynamic Island)
- Alignment with seasonal moments

**Timing:** Submit tied to a relevant moment — World Breastfeeding Week (Aug), Baby Safety Month (Sep), or a new iOS feature launch.

## Phase 2: Organic Growth (Weeks 2-8)
*Cost: £500-2,000. Goal: Build social presence and early community.*

### 2.1 TikTok + Instagram Reels (Week 2 onwards)

**Nobody in the baby tracker space is on TikTok.** First-mover advantage is massive.

**Post 3-5x per week.** Cross-post the same 9:16 vertical video to TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

**Content that goes viral for parenting:**

| Type | Example | Why It Works |
|------|---------|-------------|
| Relatable humour | "3am feeds be like..." | Shares (3x algorithm weight) |
| Real talk | "Things nobody tells you about breastfeeding" | Saves + comments |
| Day in the life | Messy, real, unfiltered day with a newborn | Watch time |
| Before/after | "Before: Googling at 3am. After: one glance at OBubba" | Problem → solution |
| Baby milestones | First smile, first food, first steps | Emotional engagement |
| App demo | Quick screen recording solving a real problem | Conversion |

**The rules:**
- Hook in first 2 seconds (bold statement, surprising fact, question)
- Raw > polished. Leave in the crying baby, messy kitchen, real life. 25% higher engagement.
- Use trending sounds (40% more likely to retain viewers past 5 seconds)
- Optimise for SHARES — the algorithm weights external shares at 3x. Make content partners want to forward to each other.
- Use hashtags: #parenttok #mumtok #babytok #newmum #firsttimemum #mumlife

### 2.2 Nano-Influencer Campaign (Week 3)

**Budget: £500-£1,500 for 10-20 mum creators**

| Tier | Cost per post | What you get |
|------|--------------|-------------|
| Nano (1K-10K followers) | £20-£120 | Authentic, high engagement, affordable |
| Micro (10K-50K) | £200-£500 | Broader reach, still authentic |

**How to find them:** Search #mumlife #newmumlife #babytok on TikTok/Instagram. Find real mums with babies 0-12 months who post organically. DM them.

**The ask:** "Hey! I'm a mum who built a baby tracker app because nothing else worked for me. Would you try it for a week and share your honest experience? I'll give you lifetime Premium + £50-100."

**What they create:** "Day in my life" showing the app in real use. Not scripted, not polished.

### 2.3 Community Seeding (Week 2 onwards)

**Free channels with huge parenting audiences:**

| Platform | Audience | Approach |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Mumsnet | 11M+ monthly visitors (UK) | Participate genuinely for 2 weeks. Answer questions. Then mention app when relevant. |
| Reddit r/NewParents | 400K+ members | Same — be helpful first. "I built this because I had the same problem" gets goodwill. |
| Reddit r/beyondthebump | Large, active | Breastfeeding, sleep, nappy questions. Answer them. |
| Facebook mum groups | 50K-200K per group | Join local new parent groups. Be a member, not a marketer. |
| NCT WhatsApp groups | Hyper-targeted | If you're in one — share naturally |

**Rule:** NEVER spam. Participate for weeks before mentioning your app. Founders get goodwill that marketers don't.

## Phase 3: Paid Growth (Weeks 6-16)
*Cost: £3,000-£8,000/month. Goal: Scalable acquisition across 3 channels.*

### 3.1 Apple Search Ads (Start Week 6)

**Why first:** Someone searching "baby tracker" on the App Store is already looking to download. Highest intent, highest conversion.

**4-Campaign Structure:**

| Campaign | Keywords | Expected CPI | Budget |
|----------|----------|-------------|--------|
| Brand | "obubba", variations | £0.50-1.00 | £200/mo |
| Category | "baby tracker", "feeding tracker", "sleep tracker", "nappy tracker", "breastfeeding app" | £2.50-4.00 | £800/mo |
| Competitor | "huckleberry", "baby tracker nighp", "glow baby", "wonder weeks" | £1.50-3.00 | £500/mo |
| Discovery | Broad match + Search Match ON | £2.00-4.00 | £500/mo |

**Total: ~£2,000/month → 500-800 installs/month**

**Rules:**
- Don't set a CPA cap for the first 14 days — let it learn
- Add negative keywords aggressively to prevent waste
- Use Custom Product Pages (up to 70) to match screenshots to search intent
- Update keywords every 2-3 weeks based on ranking data

### 3.2 TikTok Spark Ads (Start Week 8)

**Why:** Lowest CPI of any platform (£1.50-3.00). Spark Ads boost existing organic content — 30-40% lower CPA than standard ads.

**Strategy:**
1. Your nano-influencer content from Phase 2 becomes your ad creative
2. Boost the best-performing organic posts as Spark Ads
3. Target: parenting interests, #newmum, #mumlife, babies & toddlers
4. Budget: £1,500-2,500/month → 500-1,600 installs/month

**Creative that converts:**
- UGC "problem-solution": Mum describes chaos → discovers OBubba → shows relief
- 15-second screen demos with voiceover
- "I tried every baby tracker app — this is the one I kept"

### 3.3 Meta Ads (Start Week 10)

**Why:** Life Event targeting — "New Baby" — is the most powerful targeting option for reaching your exact audience.

**Strategy:**
- Reels/video ads (15-30 seconds) outperform static images for app installs
- Target: Life Event "New Baby" + Parents of infants (0-12 months) + Interest in baby products
- Use Advantage+ audiences — Meta's algorithm is now better at finding your users than manual targeting
- Budget: £1,500-2,500/month → 375-1,250 installs/month

## Phase 4: PR & Credibility (Weeks 8-16)
*Cost: £0-500. Goal: Press coverage and institutional trust.*

### 4.1 Data-Driven PR

**The hook:** OBubba collects real baby data. Package anonymised insights into headlines:

- "UK babies sleep 30 minutes less than the NHS recommends, new data shows"
- "First-time parents check their baby tracker 47 times per day"
- "Babies who nap at consistent times sleep 40 minutes longer at night"

**Pitch to:** TechCrunch, TechRound (UK startups), Forbes, Mother & Baby, Mumsnet editorial, BBC Technology, The Guardian Family section.

**The angle:** Solo mum founder builds NHS-backed AI baby tracker at 3am because nothing else existed. Now thousands of parents use it.

### 4.2 Awards & Validation

**Submit to:**
- Apple App Store Awards (annual, submit via featuring nominations)
- Mumsnet Best Awards (parenting category)
- Mother & Baby Awards
- The Webby Awards (apps category)
- UK Startup Awards

### 4.3 NHS & Institutional Partnerships

**The ultimate trust signal for UK parents.** If your app aligns with NHS guidance (which it does), explore:
- Health visitor recommendations
- NHS trust partnerships (Baby Buddy app grew this way)
- Baby Buddy/Best Beginnings collaboration

## Phase 5: Scale (Month 4+)
*Goal: Double down on what works, cut what doesn't.*

### 5.1 Measure Everything

After 12 weeks of paid ads, you'll know:
- Which channel delivers lowest CPI
- Which creative style converts best
- What your free-to-paid conversion rate is
- What your LTV is

**Double spend on the winner. Cut the loser.**

### 5.2 Brand Partnerships

Once you have 10K+ users, approach baby brands:

| Partner Type | What You Offer | What You Get |
|-------------|---------------|-------------|
| Baby gear brands (MAM, Tommee Tippee) | In-app product recommendations | Sponsorship fee + cross-promotion |
| Subscription boxes (Lovevery) | Card in their shipments promoting OBubba | Install volume |
| Baby retailers (Boots, Mamas & Papas) | Co-branded content/guides | Distribution to their audience |

### 5.3 Android Push

Huckleberry's Android app is broken (crashes, freezing, "not responding"). Their users are begging for an alternative. Once OBubba's Android app is stable:
- Target "huckleberry" and "baby tracker" on Google Play
- Run Google App Campaigns (UAC) — £1,500-2,500/month
- Content: "Finally, a baby tracker that works on Android"

If you do nothing else:

1. **Get to 100 reviews at 4.7+ stars.** Everything else is 10x harder without this. The review prompt after positive moments is your most important feature.

2. **Post on TikTok 3-5x per week.** Raw, real, unfiltered. Nobody in your category is doing this. You have the entire platform to yourself.
