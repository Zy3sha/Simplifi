# OBubba Parent Acquisition Sprint

Date: 2026-05-13
Owner: OBubba
Goal: put OBubba in front of parents who are actively looking for baby tracking, sleep, feeding, nappies, routines and calmer handovers.

## Positioning

OBubba is the calm baby tracker and parenting app for feeds, breastfeeding, bottles, naps, sleep, night wakes, nappies, weaning, growth, milestones, memory book moments, reports and Bubba Care handovers.

Short pitch:

OBubba helps tired parents understand their baby's rhythm without turning care into admin. Track the day, spot patterns, and share the care with partners, grandparents, nurseries and trusted carers.

## Priority audiences

1. Newborn parents
   - Search intent: newborn tracker, feeding log, nappy log, baby daily log.
   - Promise: remember the tiny details without relying on tired memory.

2. Sleep-deprived parents
   - Search intent: baby sleep tracker, wake window tracker, nap tracker, baby sleep consultant app.
   - Promise: see rhythm and context without rigid sleep rules.

3. Breastfeeding and mixed-feeding parents
   - Search intent: breastfeeding tracker, bottle tracker, feeding tracker.
   - Promise: keep breast, bottle, pumping and notes beside sleep and nappies.

4. Families sharing care
   - Search intent: partner baby tracker app, baby tracker for grandparents, nursery handover app.
   - Promise: fewer handover texts and fewer repeated questions.

5. Parents with unsettled, refluxy or preterm babies
   - Search intent: reflux baby tracker, colic diary, preterm baby tracker.
   - Promise: explain the day more clearly to carers or health professionals.

## What shipped in this sprint

- New SEO topic pages for UK baby tracker, wake windows, newborn feeding and nappy logs, partner tracking, grandparents, nursery handovers, reports, free tracking and routine searches.
- Six new blog posts for parent questions with official health references where relevant.
- Homepage internal links expanded to point crawlers and parents toward these new pages.
- Updated sitemap, RSS feed and `llms.txt` through the site generator.

## 14-day execution plan

Day 1:
- Submit sitemap in Google Search Console.
- Use URL Inspection for the homepage, `/best-baby-tracker.html`, `/baby-tracker-app-uk.html`, `/newborn-feeding-and-nappy-log.html`, `/baby-wake-window-tracker.html`, and `/blog/best-baby-tracker-app-uk.html`.
- Check that App Store and Google Play links work from every new high-intent page.

Day 2:
- Update App Store default product page using the ASO plan.
- Prepare 3 Apple custom product pages: Sleep, Feeding, Family Handovers.
- Prepare 3 Google Play custom store listings: Sleep, Newborn, Shared Care.

Day 3:
- Publish 3 TikTok/Reels videos from the creator kit.
- Pin the best-performing video.
- Add the best organic hook to the app store screenshot caption backlog.

Day 4:
- Send 20 personal pitches to micro-creators: newborn, breastfeeding, sleep, UK mum, dad, grandparent care.
- Offer a free premium code, not a cash-only ask.

Day 5:
- Send founder-story pitch to parenting newsletters, local press and mum communities.
- Point them to homepage, App Store, Google Play and founder quote.

Day 6:
- Create one short demo video per audience:
  - "When did baby last feed?"
  - "Grandma is babysitting tonight."
  - "Why was bedtime chaos?"

Day 7:
- Review Search Console coverage and impressions.
- Add internal links from the best blog posts to the highest-impression topic pages.

Days 8-14:
- Keep publishing one short video per day.
- Start App Store and Google Play listing experiments.
- Compare page views, store clicks and install conversion by topic.
- Rewrite the weakest hook and keep the strongest promise.

Track these weekly:

- Google Search Console impressions by page.

- App Store product page views and conversion rate.

- Creator video views, saves, shares and profile clicks.
