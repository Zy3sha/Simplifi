# OBubba Creator and PR Kit

Date: 2026-05-13
Goal: give creators, parent communities and journalists easy angles for talking about OBubba.

## One-line description

OBubba is a calm baby tracker app that helps parents track feeds, sleep, nappies, routines and milestones, then share the care with partners, grandparents, nurseries and trusted carers.

## Founder angle

OBubba was built from the 3am parent problem: too many details, too little sleep, and too much baby care living in one person's head.

Founder quote:

"I wanted OBubba to feel like relief. Not another chart judging you, but one soft place for the facts, the rhythm and the handover."
