What is SleepSense and how does it predict naps? + SleepSense uses age-based wake windows, circadian rhythm patterns, and your baby's personal sleep history to predict the best time for the next nap or bedtime. After 5 days of tracking it starts learning your baby's unique rhythm and gets more accurate over time.
