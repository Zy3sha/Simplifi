# OBubba Applications And Support Shortlist

Prepared: 2026-05-15

Goal: prioritise support that can help OBubba grow without burning the GBP 500/month budget.

Safety rule: apply first for free support, free market research and non-dilutive grants. Park anything that includes equity, ASA/SAFE, loans, match funding, repayment, exclusivity, broad marketing rights or IP assignment until the terms are reviewed.

## Priority Order

Type: free accelerator, no cash cost found.

Why it matters: it is designed for UK female tech founders with an MVP or near-MVP and user feedback. OBubba is already live, which is stronger than "idea stage."

Deadline: 2026-06-12.

Time cost: around 3 hours per week during the 9-week programme.

Use this angle:

OBubba is a live UK-built baby-care app founded by a Wirral mum after colic, reflux and sleep deprivation exposed a gap in parent support. It combines free core tracking with premium personalised nap predictions and advanced family support.

Action: review and send the prepared Gmail draft to consultancy@lyvalabs.com.

## Application Answer Bank

### What problem does OBubba solve?

New parents are expected to remember feeds, breastfeeding sides, nappies, naps, night wakes, symptoms, milestones and care handovers while sleep deprived. Existing baby apps often split the job across narrow tools, generic charts or premium gates. OBubba gives families one calm shared baby brain, with free core tracking and premium personalised rhythm support.

### Why are you the right founder?

I am the parent I am building for. My son Oliver was born with colic and reflux, and I experienced the isolation, sleep deprivation and conflicting advice first-hand. I built OBubba during maternity leave between nappy changes, naps and baby classes because I needed it myself and did not want other parents to feel as lost as I did.

### What makes it different?

OBubba is not just a tracker and not just a sleep prediction app. It combines everyday baby logs, breastfeeding, mixed feeding, sleep rhythm, weaning, growth, milestones, carer handovers and parent support in one place. It is built around the whole care picture, not one isolated feature.

### What is the social impact?

OBubba helps reduce the invisible mental load on parents, especially in the newborn phase. It can help parents organise information, share care more clearly with partners or carers, and feel less alone when everything feels chaotic. The free core tracker keeps the essential support accessible.

### What support do you need?

I need support with growth strategy, partnerships, grant readiness, parent-service feedback, privacy/safety positioning, and low-budget acquisition. My budget is around GBP 500 per month and my time is split between caring for my baby and building the business.
