# OBubba Mum Creator Outreach Sprint

Prepared: 2026-05-15

Goal: get honest mum/parent creator posts on TikTok for OBubba without wasting the GBP 500/month budget.

## Positioning

Use "founding creator partnership", not "as little money as possible."

Offer:

- Lifetime OBubba Premium.
- Early access/founder relationship.
- A small thank-you fee where needed.
- Clear option to post only if the app genuinely fits their life.
- Paid usage rights only if agreed separately.

## Creator Offer

### Option A: Gifted Founding-Parent Trial

The creator gets lifetime OBubba Premium, tries the app for one week, and posts only if it genuinely helps.

Best for:

- Nano creators.
- Local mum accounts.
- Parent bloggers who like founder stories.

Disclosure:

- They should disclose gifted access according to platform/ASA rules.

### Option B: Paid TikTok Creator Post Starter

Ask for their starter rate for a creator-page post, not a delivered-only UGC asset:

- One 15-30 second vertical TikTok posted on the creator's own TikTok account.
- Natural parent voiceover or face-to-camera.
- App screen recording.
- 1 hook + 1 CTA.
- Post kept live for at least 30 days.
- Screenshot of basic TikTok analytics after 7 days.
- Delivered video file only if included in the same budget.
- Optional raw footage add-on.
- Optional 3-month paid ad usage rights.

Best for:

- Creators with babies/newborns who can make realistic content quickly.
- Mum creators with small but real parent audiences and recent TikTok views.

## Budget Guardrail

First test budget: GBP 0-250 total.

Creator-page posting rule:

- Maximum GBP 100 per influencer all-in.
- A paid creator deal must include one organic post on the creator's own TikTok.
- Instagram/Reels is a useful bonus, but TikTok is the required channel going forward.
- Delivered UGC-only can be useful later, but it is not the current first-test priority.
- If the creator cannot include their own social post inside the GBP 100 cap, park the deal or ask for gifted/lifetime Premium instead.
- Do not newly contact delivered-only UGC creators unless they also offer TikTok posting within budget.

Preferred order:

1. Gifted/lifetime Premium TikTok posts on creator pages.
2. GBP 30-60 organic TikTok posts from newer mum creators.
3. GBP 75-100 only for a creator with clear baby/new-mum fit, recent TikTok views, and one public TikTok on their own page.
4. Do not pay GBP 100+ per creator in this first test.

## Usage Rights Rule

Important distinction:

- Delivered UGC/content means the creator sends OBubba a video file for OBubba to use.
- Paid usage rights means OBubba can run that video on OBubba's own channels or ads for an agreed period.
- Creator-page posting means the creator posts to their own TikTok/Instagram/Reels audience. This is a separate deliverable unless explicitly included.
- Going forward, "creator outreach" means TikTok creator-page posting by default.

For gifted posts:

- Do not assume OBubba can run the video as a paid ad.
- Ask permission before reposting.

For paid UGC:

- Get written agreement for where it can be used: organic social, website, paid ads.
- Ask for duration: 3 months to start.
- Ask whether raw footage is included.
- Ask whether baby face is included or excluded.

## Claim-Safe Creator Lines

Use:

- "Free to download."
- "Core tracking is free."
- "One calm place for feeds, breastfeeding, sleep, nappies, weaning and handovers."
- "Helps me stop holding the whole baby day in my head."
- "Premium adds personalised nap predictions and deeper rhythm support."

Avoid:

- "Guaranteed better sleep."
- "Cures colic/reflux."
- "Medical advice" or "NHS approved."
- "#1", "best" or "only."
- Unsafe sleep/feeding claims.

## First Video Hooks

- "3am maths is not real maths."
- "I stopped using my notes app as a baby tracker."
- "My partner does not need to ask when the last feed was anymore."
- "Left side, right side, bottle, nap... my brain was done."
- "Baby is ready for solids. My brain was not."
- "The handover should not take longer than the nap."
