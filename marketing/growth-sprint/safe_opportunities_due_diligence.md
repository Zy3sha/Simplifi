## Safe To Proceed

### Barclays Eagle Labs Female Founder Accelerator

Verdict: safe enough to prepare and apply after you review the final application.

What I found:

- Free nine-week programme.
- Supports up to 100 UK female founders.
- Requires MVP or near-MVP and user feedback.
- Requires ability to commit around three hours weekly.
- Deadline: Friday 12 June 2026.
- Programme starts in September 2026.

Watch-outs:

- It is competitive.
- It may ask whether you plan to scale/raise in 12 to 18 months. We can frame this as "funding-ready, not fundraising immediately."
- Do not tick any consent that allows broad marketing use of personal founder story/images unless you are comfortable with it.
- If final terms mention equity, success fees, commission on future fundraising, repayment or exclusivity, stop and review.

Apply angle:

OBubba is a live, mum-founded UK baby-care app with early traction, clear founder-market fit and a scalable mission: make free core baby tracking and calmer parent support accessible to families.

### LYVA Labs Innovation Consultancy

Verdict: proceed with the prepared email draft asking for the fully funded consultancy route only.

What I found:

- LYVA Accelerate is fully funded and offers commercialisation support, including up to GBP 10k budget toward expert support.
- LYVA R&D Partner Grants are not the first move: the grant route mentions a 50% business match, ideal GBP 1m+ turnover and 3+ employees.
- LYVA Accelerate excludes "consumer health" under Health & Life Science sector wording, so OBubba may or may not fit. Ask first, do not assume.

Watch-outs:

- Do not apply for a 50% match-funded grant unless cashflow can handle the match.
- Do not apply for investment unless you want equity.
- Do not describe OBubba as medical, diagnostic or NHS-approved.

Safe ask:

"Can you point me to fully funded consultancy, grant-readiness, partner matching or commercialisation support that fits a live consumer parenting app?"

### NatWest Accelerator Core Community

Verdict: safe for you to join through the app/web signup.

What I found:

- Free founder community and hub support.
- No need to be a NatWest customer.
- Terms say you own all IP you create in your business while on the programme, including work developed with input from entrepreneur acceleration managers.
- Terms say business discussions with entrepreneur acceleration managers are confidential unless you consent.

Watch-outs:

- The separate NatWest Pitch competition has separate terms. I would not enter that yet.
- Pitch competition terms I found say entries become the promoter's property, finalists cover their own travel, winners do PR/marketing, and prize winners agree not to publicly engage in promotional activities with other UK banking providers for 12 months. That is not evil, but it is a surprise if you only expected "free money."

Safe action:

Join core NatWest Accelerator community. Do not enter Pitch until we review that competition separately.

### BIPC Liverpool

Verdict: safe to contact/book.

What I found:

- Free access to business and IP databases, market research, business advisers and workshops.
- BIPC Liverpool lists bookable clinics for copyright/contract law, patents, website guidance and business tax guidance.
- Contact: bipc@liverpool.gov.uk / 0151 233 5825.

Watch-outs:

- Ask for free support first.
- If referred to a paid adviser, ask for cost in writing before booking.

Best ask:

Market research, competitor data, app/parenting category research, press lists, trademark/IP advice and a sanity check on the business model.

## Prepare But Do Not Apply Today

### Innovate UK Women In Innovation

Verdict: good future target, closed today.

What I found:

- 2025/26 round is closed.
- Published round offered up to GBP 75,000 grant funding plus 12 months of support.
- Funding was under Minimal Financial Assistance, which means previous public funding must be declared and checked against the GBP 315,000 rolling three-year limit.

Watch-outs:

- Grant terms can include eligible-cost rules, reporting, audits, project changes and repayment/withholding if you do not comply.
- We should prepare evidence now, but wait for the next official competition.

## Park Unless You Choose Equity Or Debt

### Baltic Ventures

Verdict: park.

Why:

- It includes an initial GBP 25,000 ASA equity investment.
- The programme value counts toward Minimal Financial Assistance.
- If no equity finance is raised within six months, investment converts at a pre-agreed backstop valuation.
- This may be fine for a venture-backed path, but it is not a no-strings support route.

### LCR Seed Fund

Verdict: park.

Why:

- Equity investment between GBP 250,000 and GBP 2 million.
- Requires an investment deck.
- Better after OBubba has stronger retention/revenue evidence and you decide whether equity is part of the plan.

### Start Up Loan

Verdict: park as last resort.

Why:

- It is an unsecured personal loan, not a grant.
- Requires credit check and repayment.
- Useful only if there is a very clear paid acquisition or revenue plan with downside covered.

## Red-Flag Checklist Before Any Application Is Submitted

Stop and review if terms include:

- Equity, options, warrants, ASA, SAFE, convertible loan note, advisory shares or success fee.
- Repayment if you leave, fail to complete, change direction or do not hit milestones.
- Match funding or upfront spend above GBP 0 unless budgeted.
- Exclusivity, non-compete or restrictions on using other banks, platforms, advisers or investors.
- Assignment of IP, copyright in your pitch deck, product materials, screenshots, name, logo or code.
- Permission to use Oliver's story/photo or your founder story in marketing without approval.
- Mandatory travel or in-person attendance you cannot realistically manage with childcare.
- Broad data sharing with partners without a clear opt-out.
- "Grant" language that is really debt, voucher, rebate, discount, investment or paid consultancy.
