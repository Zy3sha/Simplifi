# Payment and Final-Click Handoff

Prepared: 2026-05-15

This is the handoff for actions that require the owner because they spend money, change public store pages, submit releases, or ask for sensitive information.

## I Can Do Before Payment

- Open the correct account pages.
- Prepare paused ad campaign drafts.
- Paste campaign names, countries, keywords, text assets, and creative assets.
- Check that conversion events are selected correctly.
- Check store status and draft custom listings or experiments.
- QA screenshots, copy lengths, preview videos, and links.

## Owner Must Do

- Enter or update bank/card details.
- Confirm legal billing profile, tax, and address information.
- Confirm a paid campaign launch.
- Confirm a public store metadata submission.
- Confirm an App Store manual release.
- Confirm any creator payment, contract, or sponsored-post commitment.

## Payment Stop Points

Stop and hand over when the screen says or asks for:

- `Billing`
- `Payment method`
- `Payments profile`
- `Bank account`
- `Card number`
- `Direct debit`
- `Tax information`
- `Confirm launch`
- `Publish`
- `Submit for review`
- `Release`

## Suggested Words To Confirm

Use this exact confirmation when you want spend to start:

`Launch OBubba paid ads with a hard cap of GBP ___ total and GBP ___ per day.`

Use this exact confirmation when you want a store/public action:

`Submit/release this OBubba store change now.`

Without one of those confirmations, keep work as draft or paused.
