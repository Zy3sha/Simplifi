## Funnel Targets For 5,000 Paid Users

The current measured install-to-paid rate is too early and too low to use as the strategy. The target is to move the funnel to:

- Install to trial: 60%+
- Trial to paid: 10%+
- Install to paid: 6%+
- Monthly churn: under 7%

## Revenue At 5,000 Paid Users

Using the current new-user monthly price of GBP 7.99:

- Gross MRR: 5,000 x 7.99 = GBP 39,950
- Approx store fee at 15%: GBP 5,992.50
- Net before tax, refunds, ads and support: GBP 33,957.50/month

Annual and lifetime plans will make cash collected higher than MRR in some months, but MRR should remain the steering metric.

## Practical Milestones

### Milestone 1: 100 Paid Users

Required:

- 1,667 first opens at 6% install-to-paid
- 334 trials at 30% trial-to-paid, or 1,000 trials at 10% trial-to-paid

Main work:

- Fix paywall timing.
- Make first "wow" moment happen before the trial prompt.
- Track cohort ageing past trial.

### Milestone 2: 500 Paid Users

Required:

- 8,334 first opens at 6% install-to-paid
- About 70 to 150 first opens/day for 2 to 4 months, depending on conversion.

Main work:

- Apple Search Ads exact-intent campaigns.
- Google App Campaign with first-log optimization.
- Creator posting every week.

### Milestone 3: 5,000 Paid Users

Required:

- 83,334 first opens at 6% install-to-paid.
- At 300 first opens/day: about 278 days before churn.
- At 600 first opens/day: about 139 days before churn.
- At 1,000 first opens/day: about 84 days before churn.

Main work:

- Paid acquisition scaled only after trial-to-paid is proven.
- Creator/UGC flywheel.
- Review velocity.
- Store listing conversion tests.
