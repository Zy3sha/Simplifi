## Pricing And Feature Truth

Use this wording everywhere:

- Free to download.
- Core tracking is free.
- Parents can track feeds, breastfeeding, nappies, sleep, growth and everyday care without subscribing.
- Premium adds personalised nap predictions, deeper rhythm insights and advanced support features.
- OBubba is still a functional tracker and parent support app without Premium.

Do not say:

- "Free app" without explaining the Premium layer.
- "Guaranteed sleep."
- "Medical diagnosis."
- "Treats colic/reflux."
- "Replaces a midwife, health visitor, GP, lactation consultant or sleep consultant."
- "NHS-approved" unless formal approval exists.

Safe wording:

- "Helps parents record patterns."
- "Helps families feel less alone and more organised."
- "Can support conversations with health visitors or carers by keeping notes in one place."
- "Built from lived experience of colic, reflux and sleep deprivation."

## Founder Story

Zyesha Corran is a mum on maternity leave, originally from Fiji, who moved to the UK in 2018 and now lives on the Wirral with her family.

After being diagnosed with endometriosis and PCOS, she was told conceiving naturally would be extremely difficult, if possible at all. Becoming pregnant after her wedding felt like a miracle. Zyesha did what she has always done when something matters deeply: she researched everything. She read, planned and prepared to be the best mother she could be.

Then Oliver arrived with colic and reflux, and the newborn phase did not match the books.

Zyesha found herself isolated, sleep deprived and overwhelmed, often awake for more than 21 hours while holding Oliver so he could sleep safely. Even with a loving support system around her, she felt the gap that so many parents feel at 3am: too much conflicting advice, too many separate apps, too many basic features behind paywalls, and no calm place that actually understood the whole day.

During those nights, she downloaded every app, read every article she could find, and still felt defeated. So she started building the thing she wished existed. As she found patterns and began seeing improvements with Oliver, she made herself a promise: no parent should feel lost, alone and drowning in conflicting information while trying to care for their baby.

OBubba was built between nappy changes, naps and baby classes. It brings feeds, breastfeeding, nappies, sleep, growth, milestones, weaning, notes and handovers into one gentle baby brain for the whole family.

Today, mums message Zyesha to say OBubba has become indispensable in their parenting journey. Her goal is simple: reach every parent who needs help, especially the one awake tonight wondering what to do next.

## Audience Angles

Local family services:

OBubba is a free-to-download parent-built tool that helps families organise baby care logs and handovers. It is not a replacement for professional advice, but it can help parents arrive at conversations with a clearer picture of feeds, sleep, nappies, symptoms, notes and what has helped.

Breastfeeding and infant feeding:

OBubba includes breastfeeding support, left/right tracking, pumping and mixed-feeding context in the same timeline as sleep and nappies. The useful angle is not "feeding advice from an app"; it is "one calm record for tired parents so they are not holding every feed detail in their head."

Press:

Business support:

Creators:

## Seven-Day Founder-Led Sprint

Day 1:

- Send 4 local support/community emails.
- Post founder story video: "I built this because Oliver had colic and reflux."
- Comment helpfully in 3 relevant UK parent communities without pushing the app.

Day 2:

- Send 3 local press emails.
- Record breastfeeding tracker demo.
- Apply or register for one no-cash support programme.

Day 3:

- DM 20 UK micro-creators with babies aged 0 to 18 months.
- Post "one tired brain should not have to remember everything."
- Ask existing happy mums for a one-sentence testimonial.

Day 4:

- Follow up with local business support.
- Make one screen recording for feeds, breastfeeding, nappies and sleep.
- Turn the founder story into LinkedIn and local Facebook versions.

Day 5:

- Send 5 founder/tech press emails.
- Prepare Barclays Female Founder Accelerator application notes.
- Review App Store and Play Store conversion after any outreach spike.

Day 6:

- Build parent quote bank.
- Post "what I wish I had during colic and reflux nights."
- Create one creator whitelisting shortlist, but do not pay anyone yet.

Day 7:

- Review replies.
- Sort targets into yes, maybe, later, no.
- Pick one weekly hero metric: cost per first log for paid, reply rate for outreach, parent quote count for trust.
