# OBubba Content Feature Brief For Drew

Prepared: 2026-05-15

Purpose: give Drew a practical map of what OBubba does so he can help Zyesha create videos, UGC-style assets, screen recordings and content for ads/social. Campaign setup, media buying and testing stay with Zyesha and Codex.

## One-Line Product Positioning

OBubba is a free-to-download baby tracker and parent support app that keeps feeds, breastfeeding, sleep, nappies, weaning, growth, milestones, medicine notes, reports and care handovers in one calm place.

Core tracking is free. Premium adds personalised nap predictions, deeper rhythm insights and advanced support features, but the app remains useful for everyday tracking and parent support without a subscription.

## Main Audience

Parents and carers with babies from newborn to weaning age, especially:

- New parents doing night feeds and trying to remember everything.
- Breastfeeding or mixed-feeding parents.
- Families dealing with colic, reflux, unsettled days or sleep confusion.
- Parents who hand care between mum, dad, grandparents, babysitters, nurseries or other trusted carers.
- Families where one parent has iPhone and another has Android.

## Complete Feature Map

### Daily Baby Tracker

- One shared baby timeline/day view.
- Visual day/rhythm clock for feeds, sleep and care events.
- Quick logging for feeds, sleep, naps, nappies, medicine, temperature and notes.
- Edit/delete logs.
- Calendar/history by day.
- Daily notes and pinned important notes.
- Multiple child profiles.
- Local-first use with optional account/cloud backup.
- Import and export via CSV.
- Works as a free core tracker.

### Feeding And Breastfeeding

- Breastfeeding tracking.
- Left/right breast timers.
- Start/stop breast timers from app, Siri and widget actions.
- Bottle feeds.
- Pumping/expressed milk notes.
- Mixed feeding support.
- Feed amounts in ml/oz.
- Night feed logging.
- Bedtime feed choice: night wake feed vs morning wake feed.
- Feeding context alongside sleep, nappies, mood and growth.
- Breastfeeding guide sections for cluster feeding, supply worries, tongue tie, growth spurts and where to get support.
- Feeding insights that use wet nappies, mood, growth and pattern context where available.

### Sleep, Naps And Rhythm

- Nap tracking.
- Sleep tracking.
- Bedtime tracking.
- Morning wake tracking.
- Night wake tracking.
- Nap timers and bedtime timers.
- Wake windows and next sleep cues.
- Personalised nap/bedtime predictions.
- Today's plan.
- Sleep budget and age-aware sleep ranges.
- Schedule maker for a steadier rhythm.
- Sleep quality scoring and weekly sleep patterns.
- False-start and bedtime resistance support.
- Refused nap/rescue nap guidance.
- Nap review, including wake mood, settling time and location.
- Gentle 14-day Sleep Coach options for older babies: no-cry/gentle, chair shuffle and parent-led rhythm.
- Safe sleep guidance/signposting.
- Sound machine with white, pink, brown, rain, heartbeat and shush sounds, volume control and auto-stop timer.

### Nappies And Poo

- Wet nappy logging.
- Dirty nappy logging.
- Wet + dirty nappy logging.
- Wet nappy count in widgets.
- Poo guide for colour, texture and frequency.
- Nappy context shown alongside feeds/sleep when looking for patterns.

### Weaning And Allergens

- Weaning hub.
- First tastes/food logging.
- Food reactions: loved, good, neutral, disliked, allergic/bad.
- Allergen tracking.
- Allergen safety gate for eczema, known allergy and family history.
- Two-hour allergen watch prompt after new allergens.
- Exposure counts for repeated foods.
- Weekly shopping list.
- Recipes and ingredients.
- Tags for veg, fruit, grains, protein, dairy, allergen, iron, vitamin C, omega 3, finger food, puree, no salt, choking check and more.
- Dietary preferences such as vegetarian, vegan, CMPA, halal and kosher.
- Weaning reports and weekly planning.

### Growth, Milestones And Development

- Growth tracking.
- Milestone tracking.
- Milestone notes/photos.
- Milestone share cards.
- Development waves/context.
- Age-aware play ideas.
- Teething symptoms.
- Vaccination checklist/reference schedule.
- Memories and profile photos.
- Toddler handoff context for later-stage support.

### Medicine, Temperature And Health Notes

- Medicine logging.
- Saved medicines and dose notes.
- Medicine schedules/reminders: daily, every other day, every 4/6/8 hours.
- Temperature logging.
- Medicine dose history.
- Vaccination reference/checklist.
- Colic, reflux, CMPA, tongue tie, NICU/preterm and settling context through notes/profile signals.
- Appointments calendar.
- Appointment reminders.
- Repeat appointments.
- Optional travel-time calculation/reminder.
- Health visitor/doctor-style report generated after enough data.
- Report can be copied/shared, and on supported native builds saved/shared as PDF.

### Family, Partner Sync And Bubba Care

- iOS and Android support.
- Partner/family sync.
- Shared baby timeline.
- Invite/sync codes.
- Username and PIN account flow.
- Recovery email.
- Cloud backup.
- Offline/local data use.
- Bubba Care share guide for trusted carers.
- Carer link, no full app required for the carer.
- Carer can log feeds, naps, nappies and notes.
- Parent reviews carer entries and can accept/dismiss them.
- Emergency contacts in care guide.
- Comfort/routine notes for carers.
- Safe sleep essentials included in carer guide.

- Weekly share card.
- Health visitor/doctor report.

- iPhone widgets.

### Parent Support

- Parent Room/wellbeing check-ins.

## Claim-Safe Language

Use:

- "Free to download."
- "Core tracking is free."
- "One calm place for baby's day."
- "Helps parents track and understand patterns."
- "Designed for feeds, sleep, nappies, weaning, growth and handovers."
- "Personalised nap predictions and rhythm insights with Premium."
- "Not medical advice. Always speak to a health professional for concerns."

Avoid:

- "Guaranteed better sleep."
- "Cures colic/reflux."
- "Medical app" or "NHS approved."
- "Best", "#1", "only" unless there is proof.
- Fear-heavy messaging about baby health.
- Showing competitor logos/screenshots in ads.
