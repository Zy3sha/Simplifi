## Barclays Female Founder Accelerator

### One-Line Summary

OBubba is a free-to-download baby tracker and parent support app built by a Wirral mum after colic, reflux and sleep deprivation made it clear that one tired parent should not have to hold the whole baby routine in their head.

### Elevator Pitch

OBubba helps parents track feeds, breastfeeding, nappies, naps, night wakes, growth, milestones, weaning, notes and care handovers in one calm place. Core tracking is free, so families can use the app without subscribing. Premium adds personalised nap predictions, deeper sleep rhythm insights and advanced family support features.

### Problem

New parents are expected to remember everything while sleep deprived: feeds, breastfeeding sides, nappies, naps, symptoms, night wakes, milestones and what to tell partners or carers. Existing baby apps often split the job across narrow tools, generic charts or expensive premium gates. The result is more mental load at exactly the moment parents need calm.

### Solution

OBubba gives families a shared baby brain. Parents can log everyday care quickly, see the wider pattern of the day, and share care context with trusted adults. It supports breastfeeding, mixed feeding, sleep, nappies, growth, milestones, weaning, medicine notes, memories and handovers in one app.

### Founder-Market Fit

I am the parent I am building for. I was diagnosed with endometriosis and PCOS and told natural conception would be extremely difficult. Becoming pregnant with my son Oliver felt like a miracle, but when he was born with colic and reflux I found myself isolated, exhausted and awake through the night trying to piece together information across apps and articles. I built OBubba during maternity leave because I needed it myself and did not want other parents to feel as lost as I did.

### What Makes OBubba Different?

OBubba is not only a baby tracker and not only a sleep app. It combines the whole care picture: feeds, breastfeeding, sleep, nappies, growth, milestones, weaning, notes and carer handovers. The free core tracker keeps essential support accessible, while Premium adds personalised rhythm intelligence and advanced family support.

### Customer

Primary customers are new parents and carers of babies aged 0 to 18 months, especially sleep-deprived first-time parents, breastfeeding or mixed-feeding parents, families dealing with reflux/colic context, and households where more than one adult helps with care.

### Business Model

OBubba is free to download with core tracking free. Premium subscription unlocks personalised nap predictions, deeper sleep rhythm insights and advanced family support features. Current UK listed pricing includes monthly, annual and lifetime Premium options.

### Social Impact

OBubba reduces the invisible mental load on parents by helping families organise baby care in one place. It can help parents feel less alone, share care more clearly and arrive at conversations with carers or health visitors with a clearer record. It does not replace professional medical advice.

### 12-Month Goals

- Improve onboarding-to-first-log activation.
- Build a parent testimonial bank and case studies.
- Grow UK installs through founder-led PR, partnerships and careful paid tests.
- Build trusted local and national parent/community partnerships.
- Strengthen privacy, safety and professional signposting.
- Increase Premium conversion without weakening free core access.

### Support Needed From The Programme

I need help with low-budget growth, positioning, partnership strategy, sales/marketing discipline, funding readiness and confidence as a solo mum founder building around childcare. I want to turn OBubba from a useful app into a trusted household name while keeping the essential support accessible to families.

### Why Now?

Parents are already using multiple apps, notes and WhatsApp to manage baby care. At the same time, families are under cost pressure and cannot always pay for several subscriptions. OBubba has a live product, early traction and a founder story that resonates with the exact parents it serves.

### Commitment

I can commit around three hours per week to a focused programme, especially if sessions are planned in advance. I am on maternity leave and will need to work around childcare, but OBubba is my core business priority.
