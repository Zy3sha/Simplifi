# OBubba Creator, PR and Community Outreach Kit

Prepared: 2026-05-15

Goal: create the organic loop that paid ads can amplify.

Core truth to keep in every pitch:

- OBubba is free to download.
- Core tracking is free, including feeds, breastfeeding, nappies, sleep, growth and everyday care.
- Premium adds personalised nap predictions, deeper rhythm insights and advanced family support features.
- The app remains a functional tracker and parent support tool without a subscription.

## Creator Target List Criteria

Prioritize creators who:

- Have a baby aged 0 to 18 months.
- Post naturally about sleep, feeding, routines, newborn life, breastfeeding, reflux, weaning, or partner load.
- Have comments from real parents, not only brand giveaway comments.
- Have 1,000 to 50,000 followers.
- Are active on TikTok and willing to post OBubba on their own TikTok.
- Fit the current cap: GBP 100 max per influencer all-in, ideally GBP 25-75 for smaller accounts.
- Have recent TikToks with enough views to justify the fee.
- Are worldwide, with UK/US/AU/CA useful but not required if the parent audience is strong.
- Are comfortable showing app screen recordings or voiceover.

Avoid:

- Accounts that only post polished ads.
- Delivered-only UGC creators who will not post on their own TikTok.
- Accounts with tiny recent TikTok reach unless they accept gifted Premium or a very small fee.
- Accounts where baby/medical claims are exaggerated.
- Accounts that make unsafe sleep or feeding claims.

## Creator Brief - 3am Hook

Angle:

"Nobody wants to calculate feeds and wake windows at 3am."

Must show:

- One tap log.
- Clock/rhythm screen.
- A real parent moment: tired, checking what happened last, relief.

Suggested structure:

1. Hook: "I was trying to remember the last feed at 3am..."
2. Problem: notes/messages/forgetting.
3. Demo: OBubba log + rhythm/next step.
4. Human line: "It does not make baby sleep. It makes me feel less lost."
5. CTA: "Search OBubba."

Do not claim:

- Guaranteed sleep.
- Medical diagnosis.
- Replacement for health visitor/midwife/doctor.

## Creator Brief - Partner Sync

Angle:

"One baby. Two phones. No more asking when the last feed was."

Must show:

- Parent A logs.
- Parent B sees same timeline.
- Care handover or night shift context.

Suggested structure:

1. Hook: "My partner used to ask me what happened all day..."
2. Problem: mental load, WhatsApp, memory.
3. Demo: shared timeline.
4. Human line: "Now the baby admin is not all in my head."
5. CTA: "Search OBubba."

## Creator Brief - Breastfeeding

Angle:

"Left, right, bottles, pumps... I needed one calm place."

Must show:

- Breast tile/timer.
- Left/right split.
- Feeding support screenshot if possible.

Suggested structure:

1. Hook: "I could never remember which side we ended on."
2. Problem: half-asleep breastfeeding or combi feeding.
3. Demo: start timer and switch sides.
4. Human line: "It helped me stop trying to hold it all in my head."
5. CTA: "Search OBubba."

## PR Pitch - Founder Story

Subject: Wirral mum built baby app during maternity leave after 21-hour newborn nights

Hi [name],

I wondered if this might be a fit for a local founder or parenting story.

I am Zyesha Corran, a Wirral mum originally from Fiji. After being diagnosed with endometriosis and PCOS, I was told natural conception would be extremely difficult, if possible at all. Becoming pregnant with my son Oliver felt like a miracle.

Then Oliver arrived with colic and reflux, and the newborn phase left me isolated, sleep deprived and overwhelmed. I was often awake for more than 21 hours, holding him so he could sleep safely, while trying to piece together feeds, breastfeeding, nappies, sleep and advice across different apps and articles.

I could not find the calm all-in-one support I needed, so I built OBubba between nappy changes, naps and baby classes.

OBubba is now live on iPhone and Android. It is free to download, with core tracking free, including feeds, breastfeeding, nappies, sleep, growth and everyday care. Premium adds personalised nap predictions and advanced family support features.

My goal is to reach parents who feel as lost as I did in those early nights.

Would you be interested in a short local founder story?

Best,
Zyesha
