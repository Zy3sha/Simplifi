# OBubba Social Ad Script Pack

Created: 2026-05-02  
Use for: Meta App Promotion, TikTok Spark/UGC, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts  
Core audience: UK parents and carers with babies from newborn to weaning age  
Main goal: app downloads that become active parents, not cheap empty installs

## Winning Creative Direction

The strongest launch angle is a category comparison:

**"Stop juggling baby apps, notes, and messages. OBubba keeps baby's day in one calm shared place."**

Use this before naming competitors. It gives parents the comparison they instantly understand, while avoiding risky direct claims against identifiable apps.

## Creative Rules

- Format: 9:16 vertical video.
- Length: 12-25 seconds.
- Show the app by second 3 or 4.
- Use native-feeling UGC: real parent, real kitchen/bedroom/sofa/car seat moments.
- Put big captions on screen because most parents watch without sound.
- Use "free to start" instead of simply "free" because OBubba has in-app purchases.
- Do not show competitor logos, store pages, trademarks, or app screenshots in launch ads.
- Do not claim "best", "#1", "only", or "medically proven" unless you have objective proof ready.
- Safer comparison language: "one calm place", "instead of juggling", "built for iPhone and Android families", "designed for feeds, sleep, nappies, weaning, growth, and care handovers."

## Launch Testing Plan

Run 15 ads across 5 angles:

1. Sleep confusion
2. iPhone + Android partner sync
3. Bubba Care handover
4. Weaning and allergens
5. All-in-one comparison

First campaign setup:

- Campaign 1: Meta App Promotion - Android - optimise for install or first open.
- Campaign 2: Meta App Promotion - iOS - optimise for install or first open.
- Use broad UK targeting first.
- Use Advantage+ placements.
- Start with 3 ads per angle.
- Kill or pause ads after meaningful spend if they have weak hook hold, low CTR, or high CPI.
- Judge quality by `child_added`, `first_log_created`, `three_logs_created`, and day-2 retention, not downloads alone.

## Filming Checklist

- Record each script in 9:16.
- Film a human hook first, then app screen.
- Use captions burned into the video.
- Keep shots short: 1-3 seconds each.
- Use real parent settings: bedroom, sofa, kitchen, pram, car seat, changing table.
- Record clean app screen captures separately and overlay them.
- Keep the app visible and readable.
- End with both store options: "App Store" and "Google Play".
- Make Android-specific versions of the iPhone + Android ads.
- Make at least one founder/parent talking-head ad for trust.

## What To Avoid

- Do not lead with a feature list.
- Do not spend heavily on polished studio ads before UGC tests.
- Do not compare directly to named competitors until claims are legally checked and backed by a verifiable comparison page.
- Do not optimise for purchase too early. First prove install quality and first-use activation.
- Do not use scare tactics around baby health, allergies, or sleep.
- Do not imply OBubba diagnoses medical issues.
