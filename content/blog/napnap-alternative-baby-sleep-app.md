NapNap is a sleep-first tracker that predicts naps from positional wake windows and sells as a one-time lifetime purchase. If you are looking for a **NapNap alternative**, it is usually because you want more than next-nap timing: you want the reason behind the wakes, the whole day connected, and a coach in your corner.

Here is an honest comparison, and where OBubba fits. We make OBubba, so we have been straight about NapNap's strengths too.

## What NapNap does well
NapNap is focused and capable: positional wake-window nap prediction, offline use, a generous trial, and a lifetime price with no subscription. If all you want is next-nap timing in a clean app, it does the job.

## Why people look for an alternative
- **They want the "why."** A predicted nap time is useful, but when the night falls apart you want a plain-language cause and a fix for tonight.
- **They want the whole day connected.** Feeds, nappies and growth alongside sleep, so you can see how the day shaped the night.
- **They want coaching, not just a number.** Someone to say what to try next, in normal language.

## What to look for in a NapNap alternative
1. **Nap prediction from your baby's own rhythm**, adapting as they grow.
2. **A bedtime that moves with the actual day**, so overtiredness and early waking ease off.
3. **Plain-language night insight** naming the likely cause and the next step.
4. **Partner and carer sharing**, so nobody is guessing.
5. **A real free tier** to test on your baby first.
