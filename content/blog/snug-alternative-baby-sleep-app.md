Snug is one of the newer AI-first baby sleep trackers, and it does a lot right: "DreamTime" next-nap and bedtime predictions, wake-window guidance, WHO growth charts, shared caregiver access and widgets. If you have landed here, you are probably weighing it up, or you have tried it and want to see whether something fits your baby better.

Here is an honest look at what Snug does well, and where OBubba is different.

## What people like about Snug
Credit where it is due. Snug is "built by parents, for parents", with a clean tracker, DreamTime predictions that learn from your baby's recent sleep, caregiver sharing with a Walkie-Talkie feature, a photo journal and good widget and Apple Watch support. Core tracking is free, with premium on a subscription (and a lifetime option). If that is working for you, there is no need to switch.

## Why parents look for an alternative
- **They want the "why," not just the "when."** A predicted nap time is useful, but when nights fall apart you want plain-language reasons: was bedtime too late, was the last nap too long, is this a regression.
- **They want the whole day connected.** Feeds, nappies, growth, milestones and weaning alongside sleep, because an early nap changes the whole night.
- **They want prediction *and* coaching.** Not only a countdown, but a next gentle change to try when the day goes sideways.

## What to look for in a Snug alternative
1. **Nap prediction from your baby's own rhythm**, that keeps adapting as they grow.
2. **A bedtime that moves with the actual day**, so overtiredness and early-morning waking ease off.
3. **Plain-language night insight** that names the likely cause and the next step.
4. **Caregiver and partner sharing** so both parents and grandparents see the same picture.
5. **A genuinely useful free tier**, so you can test the predictions on your own baby first.
