Cocoo is one of the newer AI-first baby sleep apps, and it makes a strong first impression: a benefit-led promise to fix short naps and night wakes, a battery-style "energy" view of your baby's sleep pressure, and a chat coach that feels like messaging a calm sleep consultant. If you have landed here, you are probably weighing it up against the alternatives, or you have tried it and something did not quite fit.

Here is an honest look at what Cocoo does well, and where OBubba is different.

## What people like about Cocoo
Credit where it is due. Cocoo leads with the two problems that actually keep parents up: short naps and night wakes. Its energy or sleep-pressure indicator is a nice way to *see* overtiredness building, its schedule is aligned to mainstream AAP and WHO guidance, and its AI chat is friendly and gentle rather than a rigid cry-it-out program. If that is working for you, there is no need to switch.

## Why parents look for an alternative
- **They want the whole day connected, not sleep on its own.** An early feed or a short nap changes the whole night. Tracking sleep in isolation misses the cause.
- **They want the "why" tied to their own logs.** A friendly chat is helpful, but the strongest insight comes from *your baby's* real pattern, naming what changed and what to try next.
- **They want partner and carer sharing built in.** Both parents, a grandparent or a nanny should see the same picture without paying extra for it.
- **They want a real free tier** to test the predictions on their own baby before committing to a subscription.

## What to look for in a Cocoo alternative
1. **Nap prediction from your baby's own rhythm**, that keeps adapting as they grow, not just an age table.
2. **A bedtime that moves with the actual day**, so overtiredness and early-morning waking ease off.
3. **Plain-language night insight** that names the likely cause, an overlong wake window, a nap that ran short, a bedtime that drifted, and the next gentle change to try.
4. **The whole day in one calm place**, feeds, nappies, growth, milestones and weaning alongside sleep.
5. **Partner sync and carer handovers included**, so everyone caring for your baby stays in step.
6. **A genuinely useful free tier**, so you can compare it on your baby first.
