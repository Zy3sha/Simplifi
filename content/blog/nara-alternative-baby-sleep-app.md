Nara Baby is a favourite for a good reason: it is a clean, calm, genuinely free tracker that keeps the newborn days simple, feeds, nappies, sleep and not much to get in the way. If you have landed here, you probably like that simplicity but have reached the point where you want more help with *sleep* specifically: when the next nap should be, and why the nights are falling apart.

Here is an honest look at what Nara does well, and where OBubba is different.

## What people like about Nara Baby
Credit where it is due. Nara is simple, uncluttered and free, with easy logging and caregiver sharing, and it is especially nice in the early newborn weeks when you just want to jot things down without a system telling you what to do. If simplicity is exactly what you want, Nara is a lovely choice and there is no need to switch.

## Why parents look for an alternative
- **They want nap prediction, not just a log.** A record of past sleeps is useful, but many parents reach a stage where they want the app to suggest *when* the next nap and bedtime should be.
- **They want the "why" behind the wakes.** When nights unravel, a plain-language reason, an overlong wake window, a nap that ran short, a bedtime that drifted, is what actually helps you fix it.
- **They want the schedule to adapt as the baby grows**, through wake-window changes and nap transitions, without turning the day into a rigid timetable.

## What to look for in a Nara alternative
1. **Nap prediction built from your baby's own rhythm**, that keeps adapting with age.
2. **A bedtime that moves with the actual day**, so overtiredness and early waking ease off.
3. **Plain-language night insight** naming the likely cause and the next gentle step.
4. **The whole day connected**, feeds, nappies, growth and milestones alongside sleep.
5. **A genuinely useful free tier**, so you keep the low-stress simplicity while you test the sleep help.
