If you have landed here, you are probably weighing it up, or you have tried it and want to see whether something fits your baby better.

Here is an honest look at what Owly does well, and where OBubba is different.

## What people like about Owly
Credit where it is due. Owly is clean and genuinely AI-first: it predicts nap and bedtime windows from your baby's age and recent sleep, its weekly AI analysis surfaces patterns, and the PDF export is handy for handovers and appointments. If that is working for you, there is no need to switch.

## Why parents look for an alternative
- **They want the "why" in the moment, not just a weekly report.** A once-a-week AI summary is useful, but when tonight falls apart you want a plain-language reason now: was the wake window too long, did the last nap run short, is a transition coming.
- **They want the whole day connected.** Feeds, nappies, growth, milestones and weaning alongside sleep, because an early nap changes the whole night.
- **They want prediction *and* coaching**, not only a countdown and a chart.

## What to look for in an Owly alternative
1. **Nap prediction from your baby's own rhythm**, that keeps adapting as they grow.
2. **A bedtime that moves with the actual day**, so overtiredness and early waking ease off.
3. **Plain-language night insight** that names the likely cause and the next gentle step, when you need it.
4. **The whole day connected**, feeds, nappies, growth and milestones alongside sleep.
5. **Caregiver and partner sharing** so both parents and grandparents see the same picture.
6. **A real free tier** for core tracking, plus a trial so you can test the predictions on your own baby before committing.
