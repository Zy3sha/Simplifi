## What nappi does well
nappi is a clean, broad tracker that covers feeds, nappies, sleep and more in one place, with nap predictions and AI-assisted logging. It is improving quickly. If you want a tidy all-rounder, it is a reasonable pick.

## Why people look for an alternative
- **They want the "why," not just the log.** When nights fall apart, a plain-language reason (bedtime too late, last nap too long, a regression starting) is more useful than another chart.
- **They want sleep to be the main event.** An all-in-one tracker treats sleep as one tab among many. A sleep-first app makes the nap and bedtime decisions the centre of the experience.
- **They want it built around their baby.** Real babies drift off the plan, so an app that learns your baby's own rhythm beats a generic template.

## What to look for in a nappi alternative
1. **Nap prediction from your baby's own pattern**, not just an age table.
2. **A bedtime that adapts to the actual day**, so overtiredness and early-morning wakes ease off.
3. **Plain-language night insight** that names the likely cause and what to try tonight.
4. **Partner and carer sharing** so everyone sees the same picture.
5. **A genuinely useful free tier**, so you can test it on your baby first.
