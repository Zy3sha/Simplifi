## Choose the app that lowers the load

The right baby tracker app should make the day easier to understand. It should not make parents feel like they are failing a system.

Use this checklist before you commit to an app.

## Core tracking

- Feeds, including breastfeeding, bottles and mixed feeding.
- Sleep, naps, bedtime and night wakes.
- Wet and dirty nappies.
- Medicine, temperature and care notes.
- Weaning, allergens and reactions when baby is ready.
- Growth, milestones and memories.

## Sharing and reports

- Partner access or shared context.
- Grandparent, nursery or babysitter handovers.
- Reports for appointments or health visitor conversations.
- Export or summary options that are easy to read.

## Product feel

- Fast logging when you are tired.
- Calm language.
- No shaming around sleep, feeding or routines.
- Useful patterns, not overwhelming charts.
- Privacy that is easy to understand.

## Questions to ask

- Does this app help at 3am?
- Can another trusted adult understand the day from it?
- Does it keep feeds, sleep and nappies together?
- Does it still feel useful after the newborn phase?
- Does it make me feel calmer?
