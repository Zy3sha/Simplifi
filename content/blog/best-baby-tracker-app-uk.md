## The best baby tracker app should make the day easier, not busier

New parents in the UK are not looking for a spreadsheet with prettier buttons. They are trying to answer tiny urgent questions while tired: when did baby last feed, was that nappy wet or dirty, how long was the nap, what happened before the unsettled evening, and what do we need to tell the health visitor?

The best baby tracker app is the one that reduces the amount held in one parent's head.

OBubba is built around that idea. It keeps feeds, breastfeeding, bottles, sleep, naps, nappies, weaning, medicine notes, growth, milestones and family handovers together, so the baby day is easier to understand.

## What UK parents should look for

- Nappy tracking, not just diaper wording.
- Feed tracking for breastfeeding, bottles, pumping and mixed feeding.
- Sleep and nap tracking that keeps safe sleep context separate from guesses.
- Wake window support that respects baby cues.
- Reports or summaries for health visitor, GP, nursery or family conversations.
- Partner and carer sharing so the care day does not live in one exhausted memory.
- Clear privacy and no strange data selling.

## Why OBubba fits the UK parent search

OBubba uses the language parents actually use: nappies, carers, health visitors, routines and handovers. It is not trying to turn baby care into a performance dashboard. It is trying to give parents a calm place to put the facts.

That matters because care conversations often happen when parents are already stretched. A useful app should make those conversations easier.

## Evidence-aware, not bossy

Baby tracking should sit alongside trusted guidance, not pretend to replace it. Parents should follow their midwife, health visitor, GP or paediatrician for medical concerns.

For general context, UK parents can also use official sources such as [NHS safer sleep advice](https://www.nhs.uk/start-for-life/baby/baby-basics/newborn-and-baby-sleeping-advice-for-parents/safe-sleep-advice-for-babies/) and [NHS responsive bottle feeding advice](https://www.nhs.uk/best-start-in-life/baby/feeding-your-baby/bottle-feeding/bottle-feeding-your-baby/feeding-on-demand/).
